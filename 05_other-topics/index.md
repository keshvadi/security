# Emerging Topics in Security

In previous units, you learned the fundamental principles of cryptography, how data moves across networks and the vulnerabilities present at each layer, and how to secure modern web applications against common attacks. These topics form the essential foundation of computer and network security.

The field of security, however, never stops evolving. New technologies continue to emerge that change how systems are designed, deployed, and attacked. In this unit, we examine several important topics that have become central to modern security practice.

We begin with **Bitcoin and blockchain technology**, which introduced new models of decentralized trust and consensus mechanisms.
We then explore **cloud security**, focusing on the shared responsibility model, identity management, and the unique challenges of protecting systems in cloud environments.
Next, we discuss **artificial intelligence in security**, particularly the risks introduced by autonomous AI agents. We also examine **quantum cryptography**, which poses a fundamental threat to many of the cryptographic systems currently in use.
Finally, we address **privacy, legal, and ethical considerations**, which have become increasingly important as security technologies grow more powerful and pervasive.
